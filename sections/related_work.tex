\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

Importance-based CL either regularizes selected parameters~\citep{kirkpatrick2017overcoming,zenke2017continual,aljundi2018memory} or constrains gradient subspaces~\citep{saha2021gpm,farajtabar2020ogd,wang2025gorp}. Sparse LoRA and pruning likewise assume coordinate identity matters~\citep{yang2025nlotools,lee2019snip,wang2020grasp,sun2024wanda}, while random PEFT subnetworks provide a counterpoint~\citep{su2024random}. We test this assumption within a fixed LoRA pool and budget.

Attribution-Guided CL~\citep{liu2026attributionguided} is the closest LLM continual-learning mask, although its published description does not establish all details needed to classify its pool comparison. Non-zero initialization~\citep{meng2024pissa}, adaptive subspaces~\citep{wang2023olora,liang2024inflora}, gradient projection~\citep{zhao2024galore,choe2024logra}, and adapter redundancy~\citep{dieudonne2026adapter} address different settings. Our contribution is a controlled gradient-versus-random comparison within a shared zero-initialized LoRA pool.
