\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Parameter importance for CL.}
EWC~\citep{kirkpatrick2017overcoming}, SI~\citep{zenke2017continual}, and MAS~\citep{aljundi2018memory} compute per-parameter importance for regularization-based CL; a separate line constrains updates to a gradient subspace (GPM~\citep{saha2021gpm}, OGD~\citep{farajtabar2020ogd}, GORP~\citep{wang2025gorp}, Model Shapley~\citep{xu2025modelshapley}). All assume \emph{which} parameters are protected matters, and so do we; for LoRA-based CL we test whether the selection criterion within the trainable pool is consequential (\S\ref{sec:confounded}, \S\ref{sec:d1}). This complements RepT~\citep{li2026rept}, which argues parameter-space gradients are a noisy attribution coordinate system.

\paragraph{Pruning saliency.}
Wanda~\citep{sun2024wanda} and SparseGPT~\citep{frantar2023sparsegpt} score structural importance for one-shot weight removal, and movement pruning~\citep{sanh2020movement} ranks weights by drift toward zero. "Pruning at initialization" scores connections before training (SNIP~\citep{lee2019snip}, GraSP~\citep{wang2020grasp}), though such scores are largely interchangeable with a per-layer choice~\citep{frankle2021pruning}; mask-learning variants adapt a fixed backbone per task~\citep{mallya2018piggyback} and serve as CL task-specific subnetworks~\citep{kang2022winning}. Wanda importance is nearly orthogonal to gradient importance ($\rho = 0.086$).

\paragraph{How selection can matter.}
Several methods show parameter \emph{selection} can improve CL: HAT~\citep{serra2018hat} learns per-task masks, \citet{yang2025nlotools} reduce inter-task collision via sparse LoRA subspaces, \citet{wang2025piece} update only 0.1\% per task, \citet{lin2025sparsememory} keep only uniquely relevant memory slots, \citet{deng2024darex} revisit delta-parameter pruning, and \citet{su2024random} show random masking already discovers winning tickets for PEFT. Our finding does not contradict these: under our conditions the identity of the selected 10\% matters less than that 90\% are frozen.
\paragraph{CL with PEFT.}
LoRA~\citep{hu2021lora}, QLoRA~\citep{dettmers2023qlora}, and PackNet~\citep{mallya2018packnet} constrain updates to parameter subsets; \citet{li2017learning} (LwF) uses distillation, and \citet{biderman2024lora} find LoRA forgets less than full fine-tuning. LoRA-FA~\citep{zhang2023lorafa} freezes \texttt{lora\_A} and trains only \texttt{lora\_B}; we instead freeze a random or gradient-selected \emph{subset} of a standard LoRA. Variants refine the parameterization (rsLoRA~\citep{kalajdzievski2023rslora}, DoRA~\citep{liu2024dora}, AdaLoRA~\citep{zhang2023adalora}, LoRA+~\citep{hayou2024loraplus}) or enforce per-task subspace orthogonality (O-LoRA~\citep{wang2023olora}, InfLoRA~\citep{liang2024inflora}, Janus-LoRA~\citep{chen2026januslora}, MoRAM~\citep{lu2026moram}, ASO-LoRA~\citep{wang2026asolora}, SLoRA~\citep{yang2026slora}). Coordinate-level masking reduces forgetting without explicit orthogonality; PiSSA~\citep{meng2024pissa} sets $B \neq 0$ at initialization, so our B=0 mechanism is specific to standard zero-init LoRA.

\paragraph{Concurrent work.}
Most adjacent, Attribution-Guided CL~\citep{liu2026attributionguided} uses gradient attribution as a CL parameter-selection mask for LLMs, the class our within-\texttt{lora\_B} finding evaluates. GaLore~\citep{zhao2024galore} reduces gradient memory by low-rank projection, and LoGra~\citep{choe2024logra} scales data valuation via gradient projection. \citet{dieudonne2026adapter} show task-specific LoRA adapters exhibit low-rank redundancy, \citet{adewuyi2026multiple} find random sparse subnetworks suffice for RLVR, and \citet{azghan2026latentlora} propose latent-space adapters with gradient-free routing.
