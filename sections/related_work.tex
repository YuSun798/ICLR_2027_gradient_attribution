\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Importance- and saliency-based parameter selection.}
EWC~\citep{kirkpatrick2017overcoming}, SI~\citep{zenke2017continual}, and MAS~\citep{aljundi2018memory} estimate per-parameter importance for regularization-based continual learning, while GPM~\citep{saha2021gpm}, OGD~\citep{farajtabar2020ogd}, GORP~\citep{wang2025gorp}, and Model Shapley~\citep{xu2025modelshapley} constrain updates using gradient- or contribution-based structure. Related pruning methods likewise score coordinates or connections: Wanda~\citep{sun2024wanda}, SparseGPT~\citep{frantar2023sparsegpt}, and movement pruning~\citep{sanh2020movement} target one-shot or trajectory-based saliency, whereas SNIP~\citep{lee2019snip} and GraSP~\citep{wang2020grasp} score connections before training. Mask-learning methods instead adapt a fixed backbone per task~\citep{mallya2018piggyback,kang2022winning}. Together these lines motivate our question: once the candidate pool and update budget are controlled, does \emph{which} parameter-selection rule is used still matter? This complements RepT~\citep{li2026rept}, which argues that parameter-space gradients are a noisy attribution coordinate system; in our measurements, Wanda and gradient importance are also nearly orthogonal ($\rho=0.086$).

\paragraph{Sparse selection and PEFT for continual learning.}
Several methods show that restricting or selecting a parameter subset can improve continual learning. HAT~\citep{serra2018hat} learns per-task masks, \citet{yang2025nlotools} reduce inter-task collision via sparse LoRA subspaces, \citet{wang2025piece} update only 0.1\% of parameters per task, \citet{lin2025sparsememory} retain uniquely relevant memory slots, and \citet{deng2024darex} revisit delta-parameter pruning; \citet{su2024random} further show that random masking can discover winning tickets for PEFT. LoRA~\citep{hu2021lora}, QLoRA~\citep{dettmers2023qlora}, and PackNet~\citep{mallya2018packnet} likewise constrain the trainable parameter set, while LwF~\citep{li2017learning} uses distillation and \citet{biderman2024lora} find that LoRA forgets less than full fine-tuning. LoRA-FA~\citep{zhang2023lorafa} freezes \texttt{lora\_A} and trains only \texttt{lora\_B}; we instead freeze a random or gradient-selected \emph{subset} of a standard LoRA. Our result is compatible with this broader literature: under our conditions, the identity of the selected 10\% matters less than the fact that the remaining 90\% are frozen.

\paragraph{LoRA parameterization and subspace methods.}
A separate line modifies the adapter parameterization itself, including rsLoRA~\citep{kalajdzievski2023rslora}, DoRA~\citep{liu2024dora}, AdaLoRA~\citep{zhang2023adalora}, and LoRA+~\citep{hayou2024loraplus}, while O-LoRA~\citep{wang2023olora}, InfLoRA~\citep{liang2024inflora}, Janus-LoRA~\citep{chen2026januslora}, MoRAM~\citep{lu2026moram}, ASO-LoRA~\citep{wang2026asolora}, and SLoRA~\citep{yang2026slora} explicitly structure or separate task-specific subspaces. Our coordinate-level masking operates within a standard LoRA parameterization rather than enforcing subspace orthogonality. PiSSA~\citep{meng2024pissa} is particularly relevant to the scope of our analysis because it initializes $B \neq 0$; the zero-gradient confound we identify is therefore specific to standard zero-initialized LoRA rather than a universal property of low-rank adapters.

\paragraph{Closest and concurrent work.}
Most directly related, Attribution-Guided CL~\citep{liu2026attributionguided} uses gradient attribution as a continual-learning parameter-selection mask for LLMs, precisely the class of methods evaluated by our within-\texttt{lora\_B} comparison. GaLore~\citep{zhao2024galore} reduces gradient memory through low-rank projection, and LoGra~\citep{choe2024logra} uses gradient projection to scale data valuation. Concurrently, \citet{dieudonne2026adapter} report low-rank redundancy across task-specific LoRA adapters, \citet{adewuyi2026multiple} find that random sparse subnetworks suffice for RLVR, and \citet{azghan2026latentlora} propose latent-space adapters with gradient-free routing.
